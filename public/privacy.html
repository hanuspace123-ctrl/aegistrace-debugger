5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

AegisTrace categorizes cookies and local storage tokens into two groups:

  • Strictly Necessary (Non-Optional): Used to maintain secure authentication tokens (aegis_auth_token) and user credential hashes in browser memory. These do not require opt-in consent under ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3).
  • Analytical Cookies (Optional - Google Analytics 4): Used to measure aggregated interactions and error rates. These cookies (such as _ga and _ga_*) are strictly blocked by default until you grant consent via our Cookie Banner using Google Consent Mode v2.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR (European Union & UK)

Under GDPR Articles 15–22, you possess the following enforceable rights:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Correct inaccurate or incomplete account details.
  • Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (Art. 17): Permanently delete your user account, workspace data, and telemetry records. You can execute this instantly using the "Delete My Data" feature or by emailing privacy@aegistrace.com.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your telemetry reports in machine-readable JSON/Markdown format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3)): Revoke cookie consent at any time via the "Cookie Preferences" link.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a grievance with your local Data Protection Authority (DPA).

7. Your Rights Under CCPA / CPRA (California Residents)

If you reside in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act provides you with specific rights:

  • Right to Know & Access: You may request the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information collected from you.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: AegisTrace does NOT sell personal data or share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, charge different prices, or provide a lower quality of service for exercising your privacy rights.

8. Data Retention & Security

We retain account records for as long as your workspace remains active. Diagnostic logs and event traces are automatically purged on a rolling 30-day lifecycle. All network communications are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3, and passwords are authenticated using cryptographic SHA-256 / HMAC algorithms.
